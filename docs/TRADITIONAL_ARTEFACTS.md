# Traditional artefacts — why they are chain, not a content section

An artefact in Metrix is a Superstructure zone object with `artifact_keys`. It must survive ricochet: take apart, insert into another chain. If a “traditional knowledge” row does not move VVI / risk / consistency, it is text, not an artefact.

24 starters: 6 domains × safety/QoL (water-food-sleep, dwelling-heat, money-contract, attention-noise, body-load, community-handoff). Claims are **which slot moves**, never medical or legal how-to. Contested grade stays **U** and cannot raise assembly. Contra-indication on the industry raises risk and keeps Main closed.

They enter chain as `artefact_id` resources (task 1) and leave as packed artefacts / mini-app cases (task 5). `meta.process.artefacts_applied[]` carries `risk_delta` / `qol_delta`.
